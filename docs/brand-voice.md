# The Malak Estate Group: voice and tone

Derived from Marissa Lubera's own copy on her current site (themalakestategroup.com, extracted 2026-10-01) and her onboarding answers. Use it for every page, FAQ and post.

## Who's speaking

Marissa, in the **first person** ("I", "my role"), to one reader ("you"). The team voice ("we", "our") is only for mission, vision and values, which she wrote that way.

## How it sounds

- **Calm and deliberate.** Short declarative sentences, then a longer one that explains. No hype, no exclamation points.
- **Clear over clever.** She explains rather than sells: "I take the time to break down numbers, explain scenarios, and walk through choices."
- **Reassuring, not soft.** She names the worry, then the way through: "Real estate doesn't have to feel confusing or intimidating. With the right guidance, it can feel clear and achievable."
- **Grounded in experience.** Claims come from her background (years full-time with a national builder, investor herself), never from superlatives.

## Words she uses (reuse them)

clarity · intention / intentional · thoughtful strategy · preparation · informed · manageable · confidence / with confidence · clear plan · what's truly possible · empowered rather than overwhelmed · positioned and marketed thoughtfully · guided · honest / honesty · aligned with your goals

## Signature lines (her words, reuse verbatim)

- Cleveland Real Estate, Guided with Intention
- Buying or selling a home is a major decision. My role is to guide you through it with clarity, honesty, and a plan that aligns with your goals.
- A clear plan for buying with confidence
- Selling with strategy, not guesswork
- From first conversations to closing day, I focus on preparation, strategy, and support so the process feels informed and manageable at every step.
- Whether you're making a move now or simply exploring your options, I'm here to help you navigate what's next with confidence.
- Have questions or thinking about buying or selling?
- Let's Connect
- To slow down. To connect. To ground ourselves in something real. (Gather & Ground only)

## Avoid

Hype words (elevate, seamless, unlock, dream home), exclamation points, em/en dashes, invented numbers, "luxury" as decoration (use it only for the Luxury Homes service). Sign as "Marissa Lubera, REALTOR®".
