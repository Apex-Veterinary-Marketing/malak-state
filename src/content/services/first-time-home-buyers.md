faqs:
  - question: "Are there first-time home buyer programs in Ohio?"
    answer: "<p>Yes. The Ohio Housing Finance Agency (OHFA) offers programs for eligible first-time buyers, including down payment assistance of a percentage of the purchase price that can go toward the down payment or closing costs. Eligibility depends on credit, income and purchase price limits for your county. Source: <a href=\"https://ohiohome.org/homebuyers.aspx\" rel=\"noopener\">Ohio Housing Finance Agency, Homeownership Products</a>.</p>"
  - question: "Does the down payment assistance have to be paid back?"
    answer: "<p>OHFA's down payment assistance is forgiven after a set number of years if you stay in the home; selling earlier means repaying it. Check the current terms with an OHFA-participating lender. Source: <a href=\"https://ohiohome.org/news/releases/2025/programupdates.aspx\" rel=\"noopener\">Ohio Housing Finance Agency, Homebuyer Assistance Program Updates</a>.</p>"
  - question: "What is a Closing Disclosure?"
    answer: "<p>It's the final statement of your loan terms and closing costs. By law you must receive it at least three business days before closing, so you have time to compare it with your Loan Estimate and ask questions. Source: <a href=\"https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-closing-disclosure-en-1983/\" rel=\"noopener\">Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, What is a Closing Disclosure?</a></p>"
  - question: "Do I need to sign an agreement with a buyer's agent?"
    answer: "<p>Yes, before touring homes. The agreement states how your agent is paid, and commissions are negotiable. I'll walk you through it line by line. Source: <a href=\"https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-to-written-buyer-agreements\" rel=\"noopener\">National Association of Realtors, Consumer Guide to Written Buyer Agreements</a>.</p>"
  - question: "How long does buying a first home take?"
    answer: "<p>It depends on your search and the market. Once you're under contract, closing commonly takes about a month or so, driven by the lender, appraisal and title work. We'll set a realistic timeline together at the start.</p>"
