faqs:
  - question: "Do VA loans require a down payment?"
    answer: "<p>VA-backed purchase loans often don't require a down payment or monthly mortgage insurance, though your lender sets final terms. Source: <a href=\"https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/\" rel=\"noopener\">U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, VA Funding Fee and Loan Closing Costs</a>.</p>"
  - question: "What is the VA funding fee?"
    answer: "<p>It's a one-time fee that's a percentage of the loan amount, depending on your service category, whether it's your first VA loan, and your down payment. It can be financed or paid at closing, and some veterans, including those receiving VA disability compensation, are exempt. Source: <a href=\"https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/\" rel=\"noopener\">U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs</a>.</p>"
  - question: "What is VA entitlement?"
    answer: "<p>Entitlement is the amount the VA will guarantee on your loan, which is what makes lenders comfortable lending with little or no down payment. Your lender checks it through your Certificate of Eligibility. Source: <a href=\"https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/\" rel=\"noopener\">U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, VA Home Loan Entitlement and Limits</a>.</p>"
  - question: "How is the VA appraisal different?"
    answer: "<p>A VA-assigned appraiser determines the home's value and checks it against the VA's minimum property requirements. We plan offers and timelines with that in mind. Source: <a href=\"https://www.benefits.va.gov/homeloans/documents/docs/VA_Buyers_Guide.pdf\" rel=\"noopener\">VA Home Loan Guaranty Buyer's Guide</a>.</p>"
  - question: "Can I use Ohio programs with a VA loan?"
    answer: "<p>The Ohio Housing Finance Agency offers programs for eligible buyers, including its Ohio Heroes program for service members and veterans. Ask an OHFA-participating lender whether you qualify. Source: <a href=\"https://ohiohome.org/homebuyers.aspx\" rel=\"noopener\">Ohio Housing Finance Agency, Homeownership Products</a>.</p>"
